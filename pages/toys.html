Toys

A small collection of visual and interactive experiments.

  • Gravitas — Soft-body balls orbit a fixed mass and collide.
  • Ribbons — Bezier ribbons chase one another around a central sphere.
  • Orbit — Gravitating balls bounce and collide around a sphere.
  • Plane — Bouncing balls in a flat 3D rope-bound arena.
  • Terrain — A scrolling, breathing procedural contour map.
  • Waves — Sine waves travel across a 3D plane.
  • Glyphs — Font outlines rendered directly onto canvas.
  • CMYK — Overlapping circles use subtractive colour blending.
  • Raft — A 3D Raft consensus and leader-election simulation.
  • Rope — Ride an infinite bezier rope like a water slide.
  • Lines — Follow a winding 3D ribbon through space.
  • Collab — Collaborative 3D drawing across browser tabs or peers.
  • Rain — Raindrops create ripples across a pond.
  • Waves Deux — Gerstner ocean waves in a rope box.
  • Trending — Trending GitHub code becomes a scrolling pixel grid.
