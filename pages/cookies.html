Cookies & Privacy

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

  • No analytics or advertising cookies are used.
  • The site does not intentionally set third-party cookies.

What is stored

sitePrefs is a first-party cookie, retained for up to one year. It stores your visual and interface preferences, including background colour, dark mode, window positions, and settings for interactive pages. It is used only to restore those choices when you return.

The Trending code images toy may also store its generated image cache in your browser's session storage under trendingImageCache. Session storage is removed when the browser session ends.

Third-party requests

Most pages do not send data to third parties. The Trending code images toy fetches public repository content from GitHub and may use a CORS proxy if required. Those services receive the usual request data, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Clearing your preferences

You can remove the preference cookie using the Clear Cookies button in the site's settings, or clear it in your browser settings. You can clear session storage by closing the browser session or using your browser's site-data controls.
